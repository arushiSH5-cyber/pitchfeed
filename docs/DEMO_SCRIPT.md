# Demo script (2:30)

Record at 1440×900 or larger, browser zoom 100%, crew room open. Start from a fresh page load (`npm run dev`, then reload). No keys needed; with `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, the ICP label reads "live from Claude".

| Time | Shot | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | **Intake** | Hover the art so the doodles drift; point at the three proof chips. | "First-time founders have a product and no network. Pitchfeed finds their first ten customers, without cold spam." |
| 0:12–0:20 | Intake form | Show Togather prefilled, including school and past workplace. Click **Find my customers**. | "Your background is only used to find warm intros." |
| 0:20–0:28 | **Crew thinking** | Let the constellation draw itself while the six steps tick. | "Four agents on Band: Planner, Builder, Reviewer, Security. They research, draft and check." |
| 0:28–0:42 | **ICP** | Point at confidence bars and rule-outs. Click **Add** on "University service clubs". Click **Approve profile and find leads**. | "Every field has a confidence score. I can add an adjacent segment before anything runs." |
| 0:42–0:52 | **Feed** | Let the crew room messages land ("Dropped Coastline… hiring freeze"). | "Six found, two dropped because the facts didn't check out." |
| 0:52–1:02 | **Flip receipt** | Press **Space** (or Receipt). Point at verified source, checks, sign-offs, 6.1 credits. Flip back. | "Every claim comes with a receipt." |
| 1:02–1:10 | **Warm path** | Click **path** on the card. Drag to spin. Close. | "Gold is the shortest path from me to Dana, through an Bayview State alum." |
| 1:10–1:22 | **Edit + block** | Press **↑**. Type "We guarantee results." The hype check turns red and Approve disables. Click **Redraft from signal**, then **Approve and send**. | "Hype gets blocked. The redraft uses only the cited quote." |
| 1:22–1:32 | **Approve 3** | Press **→** twice more (3 sent total). Watch Sent today and credits drop. | "Only I can approve. Each one costs six credits: five to verify the email, one to send." |
| 1:32–1:42 | **Credit gate** | Press **→** on the 4th card. Credit modal opens. Click **Add 30 credits**. | "Out of credits, Security pauses enrichment. Metered by Flexprice, pay per lead, not per seat." |
| 1:42–1:52 | **Live signal** | Open **Live signals**. Click **Add to feed** on a new signal. | "The crew keeps watching. New signals arrive already verified." |
| 1:52–2:00 | **Schema approval** | In the crew room, click **Approve** on Builder's Grant node request. | "Agents can't change the graph schema without me." |
| 2:00–2:12 | **Pipeline** | Open **Pipeline**, drag a card to Replied, click **Simulate replies (demo)**. | "Every move shows whether I or the crew made it." |
| 2:12–2:22 | **Insights + Wrapped** | Open **Insights**, then **Play your week**; spin two cards. | "Reply rate, opener split test, and my week as a story." |
| 2:22–2:30 | **Activity + Usage** | Open **Activity log**, then **Usage & billing** (integrations chips). | "Every action logged with who did it and what it cost. Few, true, well-timed messages." |

Tips: signals arrive about 6s, 26s and 46s after the feed opens, and the schema request at about 14s, so they will be waiting when you get there. Use keyboard shortcuts (→ ← ↑ Space) for clean takes.
